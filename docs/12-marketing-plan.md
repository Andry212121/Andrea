# 12-Month Marketing Strategy

## Phasing overview

| Phase | Months | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-launch | 1–2 (build phase) | Supply-side recruiting, founder-led local outreach, waitlist |
| Launch | 3 | Buckinghamshire local PR + paid + organic push, referral programme live |
| Grow | 4–8 | Double down on channels that prove out CAC, content velocity increases |
| Expand | 9–12 | Begin adjacent-county SEO/content investment ahead of Phase 3 geographic expansion |

## Channel plan

### Local SEO (always-on, highest long-term leverage)
Execution detailed in [SEO Strategy](./09-seo-strategy.md). Owned by the founder
in Months 1–4; content velocity increases from Month 4 as first revenue funds a
part-time content contractor.

### Google Ads (Search)
- Launch with tightly-scoped, high-intent keyword campaigns: `[11+ tutor
  aylesbury]`, `[grammar school tutor buckinghamshire]`, `[11 plus tuition near me]`
  — deliberately narrow rather than broad-matching generic "tutor" terms, which
  are dominated by high-budget incumbents.
- Target £15–25 CPA at launch, tightening as landing page conversion improves.
- Budget: start at £1,500/month, scale only once CAC is confirmed sustainable
  against the unit economics in [Monetisation](./11-monetisation-and-projections.md).

### Facebook & Instagram
- Local parent Facebook groups (Buckinghamshire 11+/grammar school groups) are
  where this audience already congregates — organic, permission-based
  participation first (founder answering questions genuinely, not spamming),
  paid local-radius awareness/retargeting campaigns second.
- Instagram: founder-led "what to expect from the 11+" short-form content,
  repurposed from blog content.

### TikTok
- Short, practical "did you know" format content aimed at parents (search
  behaviour on TikTok increasingly overlaps with Google for this demographic) —
  e.g. "3 signs your child is ready to start 11+ prep", filmed simply, no paid
  spend until organic format/performance is validated.

### YouTube
- Longer-form: "Buckinghamshire 11+ explained" evergreen explainer, tutor
  interview series — doubles as SEO asset (embedded on Grammar School Guide) and
  a channel to build tutor-side trust ("meet our tutors").

### Email marketing
- Segmented lifecycle flows via Resend: parent onboarding (post-questionnaire,
  pre-booking nudge), post-consultation nudge, post-lesson-3 review request,
  monthly progress digest (feeds into AI Roadmap's Parent Insights feature),
  tutor application status updates, tutor payout confirmations.

### Referral programme
- **Parent referral:** referring parent and referred parent each receive a
  credit (e.g. £20 off) once the referred family completes their first paid
  lesson — avoids rewarding referrals that never convert to real usage.
- **Tutor referral:** existing tutor receives a one-off bonus (or a temporary
  commission-tier discount) when a referred tutor completes their first 10
  paid lessons — ties the incentive to genuine platform activity, not just
  sign-up.

### PR strategy
- Target Buckinghamshire local press (Bucks Free Press, MK Citizen) and
  parenting-focused regional outlets with a genuinely newsworthy angle at
  launch (e.g. "platform vets tutors the way schools vet staff" — leads with
  safeguarding, GradeHub's clearest differentiator).
- Founder positioned as a source for 11+ season commentary (registration
  deadlines, results day) — pitched around the actual Buckinghamshire 11+
  calendar (see [Grammar School Guide](../src/app/grammar-school-guide/page.tsx)).

### Content marketing
- Blog + Grammar School Guide as described in [SEO Strategy](./09-seo-strategy.md);
  content calendar mapped to the real 11+ academic year (registration reminders
  in Q2, exam-week support content in Q3, results-day content in Q4, "starting
  in Year 4/5" content in Q1) so organic traffic timing matches actual parent
  search behaviour rather than an arbitrary cadence.

### Partnerships
- Local businesses: cross-promotion with Buckinghamshire tutoring-adjacent
  businesses (bookshops, stationery/educational toy shops, after-school clubs)
  — low-cost, high-trust local distribution.
- Schools: **not** paid partnerships with grammar schools themselves
  (conflict-of-interest/impartiality risk to safeguarding trust position);
  instead, relationships with PTAs and preparatory/primary schools for
  parent-facing information sessions.

## Budget allocation (illustrative, Month 3–8 average)

| Channel | % of marketing budget |
|---|---|
| Google Ads | 35% |
| Content/SEO (contractor + tools) | 25% |
| Referral programme cost (credits/bonuses) | 15% |
| Social (organic + light paid) | 10% |
| PR/partnerships (mostly time, some budget) | 10% |
| Email tooling/testing | 5% |

## Success metrics per channel

- Google Ads: CAC vs. blended target, click-through rate on location-specific ad copy
- SEO: organic sessions to `/find-a-tutor` and location/subject pages, keyword
  rank tracking for the priority list in [SEO Strategy](./09-seo-strategy.md)
- Referral: % of new parent/tutor sign-ups attributed to referral, referral CAC
  vs. paid CAC
- PR: referral traffic + branded search volume lift post-coverage
