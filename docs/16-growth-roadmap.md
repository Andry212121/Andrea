# Growth Roadmap (Post-MVP)

Builds on the [90-Day Roadmap](./13-roadmap-90-days.md), which ends at a
Buckinghamshire launch. This document covers Months 4–24.

## Months 4–6 — Prove the model in Buckinghamshire

- Reach profitability (or a clearly understood, deliberate burn) at
  Buckinghamshire scale before spending to expand geographically — expansion
  funded by proven unit economics, not hope.
- Ship Phase 2 product surfaces: real-time messaging polish, dispute/refund
  workflow maturity, referral programme, Premium membership optimisation, first
  Mock Exam product.
- Target: 250+ active tutors, 900+ active families, positive parent NPS trend,
  confirmed LTV:CAC ratio in line with [projections](./11-monetisation-and-projections.md).

## Months 6–9 — Adjacent geographic expansion

- Expand into neighbouring counties with similar grammar school systems
  (Kent, Essex, Berkshire) — chosen specifically because the existing
  Verbal/Non-Verbal Reasoning + 11+ content and matching logic transfers
  directly, only local SEO pages, school data and tutor recruiting are new.
- Replicate the Buckinghamshire cold-start playbook (manual tutor recruiting
  ahead of demand-side marketing) per new region rather than assuming organic
  supply will materialise.
- Begin GCSE as a second primary subject vertical (larger addressable market
  than 11+ alone, and a natural retention path for existing 11+ families whose
  children progress to secondary school).

## Months 9–12 — National footprint, SEN vertical

- National expansion for online tutoring (no geographic constraint for
  online-only subjects/tutors) while in-person supply continues to build
  region by region.
- Launch the SEN specialist category: SEN tutors, speech and language
  therapists, occupational therapists, educational psychologists — a highly
  underserved, high-trust-requirement market where GradeHub's vetting-first
  positioning is a particularly strong fit.
- A-Level tutoring added as a third core vertical.

## Year 2 — Product depth & AI layer

- Execute the [AI Roadmap](./10-ai-roadmap.md) in order: Grammar School
  Readiness Score → lesson summaries → parent insights digest → practice
  question generation → study planner → progress predictions.
- Holiday revision courses as a cohort-based, higher-margin product line.
- Explore school partnerships (not grammar schools directly, to protect
  impartiality — see [Marketing Plan](./12-marketing-plan.md)) for preparatory
  and primary school information sessions and potential white-label progress
  reporting tools.

## Expansion sequencing principles

1. **Never expand geography faster than tutor-supply quality can follow.** A
   region with a search results page and no genuinely vetted tutors damages
   trust in a way that's expensive to repair.
2. **Content and matching-algorithm infrastructure is reusable; trust is not.**
   Every new region/vertical still requires the same manual vetting rigour —
   this is deliberately never automated away (see [Security Plan](./08-security-plan.md)).
3. **Each new vertical (GCSE, A-Level, SEN) launches only once the previous one
   has a stable, profitable playbook** — resisting the temptation to spread the
   (initially small) team across too many surfaces at once.

## Leading indicators to watch before greenlighting each expansion

- Buckinghamshire: tutor 90-day retention ≥ target, parent NPS ≥ target, CAC
  payback period within target (see [Monetisation](./11-monetisation-and-projections.md))
- Each subsequent region/vertical: repeat the same three gates before the next
  expansion decision, rather than expanding on a fixed calendar regardless of
  performance.
